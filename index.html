CheeCh Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

CheeCh ("we", "our", "the app") is a ride-sharing coordination app that lets users post and browse ride offers and seat requests. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use CheeCh, we collect:

  • Account information: email address and password (via Supabase Authentication)
  • Profile information: full name and phone number, which you provide during sign-up
  • Ride post content: origin, destination, departure time, seat counts, and any notes you add when posting a ride offer or seat request
  • Booking information: which ride posts you've booked seats on

We do not collect location/GPS data, and we do not process any payments — CheeCh has no in-app payment system.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your posts and contact details (phone number) to other users so they can coordinate rides with you via phone call or WhatsApp
  • To operate core app features: the ride feed, posting, and booking

Your name and phone number are visible to other signed-in users of the app, since the app's purpose is to let riders and drivers contact each other directly.

Data Storage

App data (account records, profiles, ride posts, bookings) is stored using Supabase, a hosted PostgreSQL database provider. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. We will delete your profile, posts, and booking records within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

CheeCh is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
